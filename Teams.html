Our Teams

Bogong Rugby Club is building squads that represent our region with pride, discipline and heart. From sevens tournaments to full XVs, every jersey worn carries our community with it.

Sevens Squad

Format: Sevens • Focus: Tournaments & Festival Rugby

Our Bogong Sevens Squad is the spearhead of the club – fast, physical and fearless. Competing in regional sevens tournaments, the squad focuses on high-intensity fitness, smart support play and playing with flair.

Ideal for players who love space, speed and testing themselves in short-format rugby.

Tournament Squad

Men's XV

Format: XVs • Focus: Structure, Set Piece, Club Culture

Our Men's XV is the engine room of the club’s long-term vision. As we grow, this side will anchor our involvement in regular competitions and build a strong Bogong identity week in, week out.

A home for forwards who love the grind and backs who want to control territory and tempo.

Building Squad

Women's XV / Sevens

Format: XVs & Sevens • Focus: Growth & Inclusion

Bogong is committed to creating space for women’s rugby in our region. As interest grows, we will shape a dedicated women’s squad, starting with sevens and building towards a full XV.

Open to all experience levels – from first-timers to seasoned players returning to the game.

Women in Rugby

Juniors & Development

Format: Skills & Modified Games • Focus: Future Bogong Players

Our juniors are the future of Bogong Rugby Club. As the club grows, we aim to offer age-based programs, clinics and fun rugby experiences that focus on skills, mateship and confidence.

Perfect for families who want their kids involved in a positive, structured sporting environment.
