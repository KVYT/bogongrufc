Your first session

What to expect

You do not need to know every rugby rule before training. Come along, meet the group and take things at your own pace.

What to bring

Wear comfortable active clothing and bring a water bottle. Ask the club about boots, mouthguards and any other equipment.

What training includes

Sessions may include warm-ups, passing, running lines, fitness, game skills, team drills and recovery work.

Who can attend

New players, returning players and experienced rugby players are welcome. Contact the club if you have questions about the right pathway.
