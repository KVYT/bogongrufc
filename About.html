Our club

About Bogong Rugby Club

We are building a proud, inclusive rugby community in the high country — bringing people together through competition, culture and connection.

Stronger together. Bogong forever.

Bogong Rugby Club is more than a team. It is a place for players, families, volunteers, supporters and local partners to belong, contribute and grow together.

Our story

Rugby with purpose

Bogong Rugby Club was founded with a simple purpose: to bring our community together through the power, passion and spirit of rugby.

What began as a small group of dedicated people has grown into a proud and determined club representing the Bogong region with heart, discipline and unity.

We believe rugby creates more than athletes. It builds confidence, leadership, friendships and a stronger connection to community. On the training paddock and during tournament weekends, members support each other to improve both on and off the field.

Our inaugural tournament in Tallangatta was an important early milestone. It showed what can happen when players, volunteers, supporters and local communities come together around a shared vision.
