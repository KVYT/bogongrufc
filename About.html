Our story

More than a team

Bogong Rugby Club was founded with one purpose: to bring our community together through the power, passion and spirit of rugby.

What began as a small group of dedicated players has grown into a proud and determined team representing the Bogong region with heart, discipline and unity.

We embrace a culture built on respect, hard work and community. Whether on the training paddock or on tournament day, our players push each other to become better athletes, teammates and leaders.

In our first year, we hosted our inaugural tournament in Tallangatta — an important milestone in the club's emerging journey. We are building a legacy that future generations can be proud of.
