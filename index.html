Our Story

Founded with passion, growing with pride.

Bogong Rugby Club was established to bring the community of the high country together through the sport we love. Over the years, we've developed from a local club into a thriving hub for rugby enthusiasts of all ages and abilities.

Our commitment to excellence, both on and off the field, defines everything we do. We believe in developing not just skilled players, but good people who contribute positively to our community.
