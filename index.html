Our story

More than a team

Bogong Rugby Club was founded to bring our community together through the power, passion and spirit of rugby.

What began as a small group of dedicated people has grown into a proud and determined club representing the Bogong region with heart, discipline and unity.

We value respect, hard work and connection. On the training paddock and on tournament day, we push each other to become better athletes, teammates and leaders in our community.

Our club values
